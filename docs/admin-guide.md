# Using Exception Reports

This guide is for owners and operators who decide how errors are routed. Every step uses the labels you see on screen.

## What you're looking at

| Screen or signal | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| **Exception reports** | The technical errors recorded, with when they happened |
| **Reported at** | When an error occurred |
| Notification settings | Who is told about errors, and how |

## What to do when...

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A new error is reported | Share the report with your developer; the detail is for them |
| Errors keep recurring | Flag the pattern to your developer as a priority |
| The right person isn't being told | Set the recipient in the notification settings |
| You get too many notifications | Ask your developer to adjust the rate limit |

## Settings and retention

- Set the **recipient** (and webhook, if used) so reports reach the right place.
- The content of a report is technical. Your job is to make sure it reaches a developer, not to fix it yourself.
